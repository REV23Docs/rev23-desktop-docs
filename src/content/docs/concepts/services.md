Services represent a single instance of a customer being tattooed or pierced in your studio. This is arguably the most important piece of the REV23 Desktop core. Along with the record of the customer, date and time, service type, and employee, a service record stores the release form, commissions, and other valuable information. For each service that is performed, you need to create a service record. That means if a customer is getting their lobes pierced and a tongue piercing in the same session, you still need to create two individual service records.

When a customer receives multiple piercings during the same visit, use a [Service Group](/rev23-desktop-docs/concepts/service-groups/) to batch-create and start the individual Service records instead of creating them one by one.
